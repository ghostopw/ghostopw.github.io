About the Author

An intimate profile, in his own words

⁂

You can call me Gabriel. Online, I go by ghostopw. I'm an AI Engineer and Computer Engineering student at IESB, based in Brasília, Brazil.

At CIIA (Centro Integrado de Inteligência Artificial) I build AI solutions for public security — from voice pipelines with ASR and TTS to conversational assistants powered by LLMs.

Before that, I spent nearly two years at Outlier AI, training and evaluating language models, automating business processes, and extracting insights with Python, SQL, and Power BI.

What drives me

I enjoy the full cycle: understanding the problem, prototyping fast, iterating with real data, and shipping something that works in production. I move comfortably between AI, web development, and infrastructure — Docker, Flutter, React, FastAPI, and whatever else the project demands.

On the academic side

I authored a paper on the Minimax algorithm applied to board games, bridging classical heuristic search with modern approaches like AlphaZero. I also took first place at the Big Data Hackathon with an end-to-end predictive-modelling solution.

Off the keyboard

I'm into PC building, gaming, barbecue, and experimenting with cocktail recipes. Born and raised in Brasília.
